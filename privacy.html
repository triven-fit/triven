TRIVEN

Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Triven (triven.fit) is a training log for swimming, cycling, running and multisport. This page explains what we collect, why, who can see it and how to have it deleted. Questions: hello@triven.fit.

Who runs Triven

Triven is owned and developed by an independent developer. Contact: hello@triven.fit.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, the name you choose to show, your password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider) and an optional profile photo.
  • Your settings: heart-rate thresholds, resting and maximum heart rate, weight, FTP, weekly goals and privacy choices.
  • Your activities: the files you upload or that a service you connect sends us (for example .FIT files) and what they contain: time, GPS location, distance, speed, elevation, heart rate, power, cadence, running dynamics, laps and device names. We also store figures we work out from them, such as training load and best efforts.
  • What you add: activity names, descriptions, private notes, photos and videos, gear, comments, kudos, follows, clubs (including club photos and messages admins post) and relays you join.
  • Feedback: suggestions and problem reports you send, with the page you were on, the app version, your browser and screen size for problem reports, and any reply.
  • Usage counts: the days you open the app (to count active members), and a daily count of visits to each page, with each browser counted once a day. Visit counts contain no personal data, no IP addresses and no cookies.
  • Connected services: if you connect another service (for example intervals.icu or Garmin), the access key or token needed to fetch your activities.

The person who runs Triven can see totals and trends (members, activity counts, visits) and the list of members' names, usernames and email addresses, to run and improve the service. We don't use advertising or third-party analytics, we don't set tracking cookies, and we never sell or rent your data. Your browser keeps a sign-in token so you stay signed in.

Why we use it

Only to run Triven for you: storing and showing your training, working out the figures and charts, and sharing activities with the people you choose. Heart rate and other health-related data are processed only because you upload them for this purpose, and you can delete them at any time.

Who can see your data

  • Each activity has its own visibility: Only me, Followers (your followers and club mates), Club (club mates) or Everyone (anyone signed in to Triven). You set a default for new uploads in Settings and can change any activity with one click.
  • For each activity you can also hide specific data from others: the map, heart rate, power, pace, cadence, running dynamics and the description. Hidden data is removed before it reaches anyone else's browser.
  • Private notes are only ever visible to you.
  • Your display name and profile photo can be seen by people you share activities with, people you follow or who follow you, and club mates. Profile photos are stored at a public address so they can be shown throughout the app. You can choose not to appear in athlete search, and to approve followers before they can follow you.
  • You can also limit who sees any of your activities to just your followers, or your followers and club mates, whatever each activity is set to. If you hide yourself from search, your activities are never shown to everyone.
  • Clubs: members see each other's names and photos, the member list and messages from the club's admins. Club admins see requests to join. Club names, descriptions and photos of listed clubs can be seen by anyone signed in; club photos are stored at a public address.
  • Relays: when you accept a relay, your leg's sport, start time, time and distance are shown on the relay to anyone who can see it. Everything else about your activity follows its own sharing settings. You can leave a relay at any time.

Where it's stored, and the services we use

  • Supabase stores your account, activities and files (database and file storage hosted on Amazon Web Services in Tokyo, Japan) and handles sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages serves the Triven website.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather shown on outdoor activities. When an activity needs it, your browser sends Open-Meteo a rounded location (to about 10 km) and the date, nothing else: no name, account or activity details.
  • Resend sends Triven's account emails (confirming your email address, resetting your password), from servers in Tokyo, Japan. It receives your email address and the message we send you.
  • Map tiles come from Esri and OpenTopoMap, and app code libraries from jsDelivr. When your browser loads them, those providers see your IP address and, for maps, the area being shown.
  • Services you connect (such as intervals.icu or Garmin) send us your activities under their own privacy policies.

These providers work on our behalf or deliver content to your browser. We don't give them your training data for their own purposes.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete any activity, photo, comment or follow at any time; deleting an activity removes its files. To delete your whole account and everything in it, use Settings → Your data → Delete my account: it happens immediately. If you can't sign in, email hello@triven.fit from your account's address and we'll do it within 30 days. If you disconnect a service, we delete its access key.

Your rights

You can download a copy of your data yourself (Settings → Your data → Download my data, optionally with your original activity files and photos), or ask us for one. You can also ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent by deleting it. Depending on where you live (for example under UK or EU data protection law) you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Triven isn't intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app about any significant change.
