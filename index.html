Welcome to Triven

Start here: choose the name other athletes see, add a photo and decide who can find you and see your training. Then set your threshold heart rates, which your zones and training load depend on. You can change all of this later. Click Apply when you’re done.

Zones, training load and estimates use these. Past activities keep the values that were in effect on their day.

Profile and privacy

Followers see activities set to Followers or Everyone. Club mates see Followers and Club activities too. “Who can see my activities” is an upper limit on top of that; a hidden profile is never open to everyone. You can change any activity with one click on its page.

Swimming

CSS (critical swim speed) is the pace you could hold for about 30 minutes, your swimming threshold. Swim a hard 400 m, rest, then a hard 200 m, and enter both times: CSS = (400 m time − 200 m time) ÷ 2. It sets your swim zones and swim training load.

When changes apply

Threshold heart rates, max and resting HR, FTP and weight are kept as a dated history. Every activity is worked out with the values in effect on its day, so changing them now doesn’t alter past activities.
Before your first dated change, each activity uses what your watch recorded in that file (heart rate thresholds and weight), if it recorded anything. FTP always comes from here or the ride itself, never the watch.
